brain-mni152.sdf.gz is derived from the MNI ICBM152 2009a nonlinear
symmetric template (grey- and white-matter probability maps), converted to
a signed distance field by scripts/build-brain-sdf.py.

Fonov VS, Evans AC, McKinstry RC, Almli CR, Collins DL. Unbiased nonlinear
average age-appropriate brain templates from birth to adulthood.
NeuroImage, 2009; 47 Suppl 1: S102.
https://www.bic.mni.mcgill.ca/ServicesAtlases/ICBM152NLin2009
